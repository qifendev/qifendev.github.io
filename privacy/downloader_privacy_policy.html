iDownload Privacy Policy

Package site.qifen.downloader · Last updated: 2026-11-15

In short: iDownload is a download manager. The files you download and your download history are stored locally on your device; we never see their contents. The app is free and supported by advertising, and uses analytics to improve the experience; see below for what is collected and shared.

What the app handles on your device

  • Download tasks, settings and file metadata are stored locally on your device.
  • Downloaded files are saved to your device storage; we do not access or upload their contents.
  • Your data is removed when you uninstall the app.

What we collect and share

To operate the app and serve ads, the following data is processed by our service providers:

  • Advertising: Google AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) and coarse device information (device model, OS version, general region) to serve and measure ads and for anti-fraud.
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics collects anonymous usage events (such as which features are used and crash-free sessions) to help us improve the app. Firebase Crashlytics collects anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs.

These identifiers and events are transmitted to Google's servers as part of standard AdMob and Firebase operation. They do not include the contents of the files you download, your name or your email.

Advertising choices

  • On iOS, you are asked for permission to track before any personalised ads are shown. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are displayed and no tracking identifier is shared.
  • We do not share the files you download or your download history with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics

Permissions

  • Storage: required to save downloaded files to your device.
  • Network access: required to download files from the internet.
  • Notifications: used to show download progress and completion; can be turned off anytime.
  • Foreground service: used on Android to keep downloads running while the app is in the background.

Data security

Your downloaded files and download history remain on your device. We recommend exercising caution when downloading files from unknown sources.

Delete your data

Delete downloads and clear history from within the app, or uninstall the app, to remove all local data. Contact: qifendev@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents or guardians can review and delete any information from the device.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new policy on this page with a new date above.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at qifendev@gmail.com.
