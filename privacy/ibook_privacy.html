iBook Privacy Policy

Package site.qifen.ibook · Last updated: 2026-11-06

In short: iBook is a local-first tool. Your files, records and settings stay on your device. There are no accounts, no personal-data collection and no ads.

What the app handles

  • Files, documents, images, recordings and notes you choose or create are read and written only on your device.
  • App settings and your recent-items list are stored locally and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • To perform its functions the app may access your local network; this never sends your files off the device.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is required, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.
  • We do not integrate ad SDKs and do not share your content with third parties for marketing.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the cloud by default. If cloud sync is added in a future version, it will be an explicit, clearly labelled, opt-in option.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions its features need (such as file access, camera, microphone and local network). You can revoke them at any time in your system settings; denying a permission only affects that feature.

Delete your data

Clear items from the in-app Settings page, or simply uninstall the app, to delete all local data. Contact: qifendev@gmail.com.

Changes

Policy updates are published on this page with a new date above. Material changes are also noted in the app.
