iDoc Privacy Policy

Package site.qifen.idoc · Last updated: 2026-11-15

In short: iDoc is a local-first tool. Your files, records and settings stay on your device. There are no accounts and we do not collect the files or content you handle. The app is free and supported by advertising; see the Advertising section for what our ad partner processes.

What the app handles

  • Files, documents, images, recordings and notes you choose or create are read and written only on your device.
  • App settings and your recent-items list are stored locally and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • To perform its functions the app may access your local network; this never sends your files off the device.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in is required, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or location.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share the files or content you handle with third parties for marketing.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the cloud by default. If cloud sync is added in a future version, it will be an explicit, clearly labelled, opt-in option.

Advertising

This app is free and supported by advertising served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and for anti-fraud, AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) and coarse device information such as device model, OS version and general region. We also use Firebase Analytics to understand app usage. None of this includes the files or content you handle in the app.

  • On iOS, you are asked for permission to track before any personalised ads are shown. If you decline, only non-personalised ads are displayed and no tracking identifier is shared.
  • We do not share the files or content you handle with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions its features need (such as file access, camera, microphone and local network). You can revoke them at any time in your system settings; denying a permission only affects that feature.

Delete your data

Clear items from the in-app Settings page, or simply uninstall the app, to delete all local data. Contact: qifendev@gmail.com.

Changes

Policy updates are published on this page with a new date above. Material changes are also noted in the app.
