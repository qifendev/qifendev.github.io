iShare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the iShare app ("Application") for mobile devices, provided by qifen ("Service Provider") as a free service, used "AS IS".

In short: iShare transfers files directly between your device and the receiving device — peer-to-peer, without passing through our servers. We do not store the text, images or files you transfer. We only collect the device and diagnostic information needed to keep the app working and improve the experience.

1. What information we processInformation we process

1.1 Device & identifiers

  • Device model, OS version, app version, language and region.
  • A randomly generated in-app device identifier used to discover other devices on the same local network (regenerated when the app is uninstalled).
  • The device nickname you set in the app.

1.2 Local network

  • To discover nearby devices and establish connections, the app needs access to the local network (same Wi-Fi) and reads device-discovery broadcasts within the LAN.
  • We do not collect the Wi-Fi names or passwords you have connected to, or your precise location.

1.3 Content you choose to send

  • When you send photos, videos or files, the app reads the files you select (requiring photo/file access permission) solely to send them.
  • Received files are stored in the app directory on your device and can be managed or deleted by you.
  • This content never passes through our servers and we cannot view it.

1.4 Diagnostics & ads

  • Crash logs and anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics) to identify crashes and improve features.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA/GAID) and coarse device information for serving and anti-fraud. On iOS, you are asked about tracking first; declining shows only non-personalised ads.

2. PermissionsPermissions

  • Local network: to discover and connect to other devices on the same Wi-Fi (core feature).
  • Photos / Files: read only when you choose content to send.
  • Notifications: to alert you when you receive a file or connection request (can be turned off anytime).
  • Tracking (iOS): for ad personalisation only; declining does not affect other features.

3. How we share informationSharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it:

  • With third-party processors that provide services to us (Firebase, Google AdMob), limited to the diagnostics and advertising data described above.
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of users and the public.
  • When you deliberately share content to other apps via the system share sheet.

4. Data retention & deletionRetention & deletion

  • Transferred content exists only on the sending and receiving devices; we cannot access it.
  • Crash and statistics logs expire automatically within Firebase's default retention window (typically 90 days).
  • You can delete chat history and received files in the app, or clear app data in system settings.
  • To delete account-related data tied to us, email qifendev@gmail.com; we will handle requests within 30 days.

5. ChildrenChildren

iShare is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe such data has been collected, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. SecuritySecurity

LAN transfers happen directly on your local network. Please use the app on trusted networks and avoid transferring sensitive files over public Wi-Fi. We continue to improve the protections around transfers.

7. Changes to this policyChanges

This policy may be updated from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date above. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Your rights

You may stop all collection by uninstalling the app, clear chats and received files in-app, or request deletion of related data via qifendev@gmail.com. Requests are handled within 30 days. We never sell personal data.
